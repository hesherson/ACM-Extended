# B319 clinical clocks and IV workflow

In CBA Addon Options under **Server → ACM Extended: Persistence → Clinical timing**, set **Drug and blood clock** to **Real-life time** or **In-game time**, then restart the mission. Real-life time is the default. In-game time uses the server's time multiplier. At 4×, two minutes of elapsed play age a drug or blood unit by eight minutes. The server integrates rate changes without jumping the clinical clock when an administrator changes the calendar. The independent **Real-time blood storage** option selects the reference or compressed blood-storage windows from B318. Leave it enabled to keep the published windows in either clock mode.

Medication onset, washout and residual amount, fresh-donor age, blood exposure, spiked-bag expiry, cooler holdover and fridge shelf life share the selected clock. Transfusion volume delivery, patient maintenance cadence, restocking, and transaction retry delays retain their own clocks. Restocking remains on the mission calendar. Campaign adapters can supply offline seconds in the selected clock's units. Use the actual elapsed wall time in Real-life mode and the intended simulated elapsed time in In-game mode. Older portable saves already store ages or time offsets, so those ages rebase to the new mission's clock.

Opened deployed coolers show the current physical cargo. A successful medical-menu draw briefly opens the cooler and removes one visible bag. Automatic LifeWarmer Quantum is part of the blood Y-set and stays on its shared outlet through saline priming, flushing and bag changes. Manual stand-alone warmers use their existing physical item and return workflow.

An IV Start Kit occupies one inventory slot entry while sealed. Its two 18G IV catheters, reusable BOA band, Tegaderm, saline lock and 10 mL saline flush count toward ACME's treatment tools. A loose supply takes priority. If treatment uses a packed disposable supply, the kit opens and issues the rest as loose items in its original container. Applying the BOA also opens the kit and keeps the reusable band. Pickup, Arsenal and tray selection leave the kit sealed. The older classnames in existing loadouts provide the same contents. Failed completed patency checks draw the existing gauge-specific bruise art at the catheter site. Successful flushes credit the normal 10 mL and leave no new bruise.
