# B317 medical equipment

Select Body in the medical menu, then Advanced and the appropriate Tempus group. Set up a monitor explicitly or choose a sensor to place and connect a carried monitor in one action. A free monitor in range can be used where it was manually placed. Choose the right arm for SpO₂ and NIBP, or the head for EtCO₂. The LS connects its own ECG leads and defibrillator pads. Pairing and USB setup require both monitors assigned to the patient.

Use ACCUVAC in the airway menu. The suction screen opens after placement is confirmed. Selecting the powered suction in the laryngoscopy tray and draining an existing chest tube use the same device. One physically deployed pump can be reused. Medic and patient items follow the mission's equipment-sharing order. Ground actions remain available for picking up and rotating equipment. Ventway still uses its patient connection and recovery actions. Set Down Blood Cooler on the Body opens the carrier selector and puts the selected cooler beside the patient.

Acceptance in Arma

- Set up each Tempus from the medical menu with a powered monitor in inventory, then repeat with a field monitor. Confirm inventory, assignment, separate placements and the selected sensor.
- Check a perfusing patient, asystole, and recovery on both displays. Review the Pro 12-lead page and pace through several sweeps with changing heart rates. Compare the displayed rhythm with the LifePak.
- Rotate each monitor with its leads connected. Verify that the lines follow the rear housing and stay slack with no upright segments. Unplug USB, remove sensors and pick up the devices. Repeat with a second client watching.
- Use ACCUVAC from the medical menu and during laryngoscopy. Verify automatic placement beside the head, a straight tip and reuse after the first opening. Remove the pump during use and confirm suction stops.
- Deploy one of two carried coolers and make sure its capacity and contents match the selected entry. Connect and disconnect Ventway normally.
