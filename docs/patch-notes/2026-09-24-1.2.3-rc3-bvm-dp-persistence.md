# ACM Extended 1.2.3 RC3 — BVM / CPR Direct Pressure Persistence

Date: 24 September 2026  
Build: B146  
Debug revision: rc3

## Runtime corrections

- BVM no longer destroys an existing Direct Pressure episode held by the same provider on the same casualty.
- Direct CPR and BVM starts both yield Direct Pressure before taking provider animation ownership.
- Direct Pressure keeps its episode worker, input ownership, target and elapsed-clot bookkeeping while CPR/BVM are active.
- Its visible provider hold and synchronized clinical pressure marker are suppressed for the full higher-priority maneuver lifetime.
- The DP worker now honors both the provider-local and patient-owner CPR/BVM handoff deadlines, so the pressure pose/marker cannot flash back during the BVM -> CPR delayed swap.
- When the maneuver family and transfer window end, Direct Pressure resumes from the existing episode automatically.
- The normal BVM treatment bridge applies the same DP pause contract even when chest-access preparation is skipped because no carrier/Semi-Fowler transition is needed.
- Direct/scripted CPR and BVM entry points enforce the same contract, so bypassing the ACE medical-menu wrapper does not reintroduce the race.

## Preserved RC2 behavior

- CPR and every BVM variant reuse one stable chest-access lease across middle-mouse swaps.
- The casualty cannot be re-dressed during CPR/BVM or the bounded transfer window.
- Semi-Fowler remains suspended while higher-priority CPR/BVM patient ownership is active.
- Chest-access preparation remains one-click with the medical menu closed and a top-center **Preparing...** state.
- Canceling preparation releases only that generation and returns to the medical menu.
- Carrier restoration remains accelerated with the current 0.75 s lift, 0.02 s hold, 0.88 s lower timing and token-scoped 1.60x patient animation speed.

## Acceptance focus

Validate on a locally hosted client and a dedicated-server non-host client:

1. Hold Direct Pressure, start CPR, and confirm pressure is not destroyed.
2. Stop CPR and confirm the same pressure episode resumes.
3. Hold Direct Pressure, start BVM, pause/resume BVM, stop BVM, and confirm pressure stays yielded until the final stop then resumes.
4. Swap CPR -> BVM -> CPR repeatedly with middle mouse and confirm the plate carrier never returns between maneuvers.
5. Repeat the swap with Direct Pressure already active and confirm neither the DP pose nor pressure marker flashes during the handoff gap.
6. Repeat from Semi-Fowler and confirm the casualty stays flat during CPR/BVM and re-elevates only after the maneuver family has fully ended.
7. Spam-click CPR/BVM while **Preparing...** is visible and confirm only one preparation generation runs.
8. Cancel **Preparing...** with Escape/F0 and confirm the medical menu returns normally.
