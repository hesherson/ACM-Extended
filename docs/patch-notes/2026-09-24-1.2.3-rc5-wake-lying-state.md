# ACM Extended 1.2.3 RC5 — Wake Into Lying State

Date: 24 September 2026  
Build: B148  
Debug revision: rc5

## Behavior

- Eligible casualties who wake on foot now become conscious in `ACM_LyingState`.
- They do not immediately play the ordinary ACE prone/get-up transition.
- `Get Up` remains a separate action after the wake.
- Ammonia inhalant, Slap Awake, Shake Awake, spontaneous wake, fracture-pressure wake, sedation washout and other canonical `requestWake` callers share this posture rule.
- Vehicle wake behavior is unchanged.

## Ordering

ACE `setUnconsciousState(false)` updates the unconscious flag and calls `setUnconsciousAnim(false)` before it publishes `ace_unconscious`. RC5 therefore marks `WasTreated` and `Lying_State` before the WakeUp event. The existing ACM wake animation sees that state and selects `ACM_LyingState`; the normal ACM unconscious-change handler then retains the lying state and presents Get Up.

## Acceptance

1. Wake a stable unconscious casualty with ammonia and confirm they remain lying after consciousness returns.
2. Repeat with Slap Awake and Shake Awake.
3. Repeat with Direct Pressure over a confirmed fracture.
4. Confirm the casualty receives the normal Get Up interaction rather than standing automatically.
5. Confirm Get Up actually releases `ACM_LyingState` and plays the normal recovery animation.
6. Confirm a casualty waking inside a vehicle uses the normal vehicle awake animation.
7. Confirm sedation, paralysis, active seizure and cardiac arrest still block inappropriate waking.
