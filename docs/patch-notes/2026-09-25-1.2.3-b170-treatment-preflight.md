# ACM Extended 1.2.3 B170

## Root cause of the "visible menu, dead buttons" failure

The runtime RPT captured the decisive state after Narc Box:

- the Narc Box treatment started and succeeded;
- its continuous-action controller then ended;
- several seconds later ACME logged that it had to clear a **stale treatment preflight**.

That means the remaining lock was not the renderer itself. The renderer could be alive and repainting while
`ACME_treatmentPreflightActive` still acted as a global treatment mutex.

The race is most visible after Narc Box/CPR/continuous-action returns because the medical menu immediately begins its
post-treatment provider pose. A new treatment click could land during that pose transition. The treatment bridge then
started a second empty-hands/crouch preflight and waited for a specific transient animation frame. If that presentation
handoff did not settle, the global preflight Boolean blocked later treatment clicks until the provider-state watchdog
eventually repaired it.

Direct Pressure bypassed that generic preflight, which is why it still worked. A longer stock treatment such as a
tourniquet also gave the stale-state watchdog enough time to recover, making the rest of the menu appear to wake up.

## B170 ownership rule

Treatment preflight is now presentation-only.

- A live post-treatment medical-menu pose is a valid direct handoff even if `animationState` has not reached its
  final generic crouch on that exact frame.
- `ACME_treatmentPreflightActive` is no longer a hard "reject every later click" mutex.
- A new accepted click supersedes an older presentation-only preflight generation. Old callbacks are token-guarded and
  become inert.
- Both weapon-prep and crouch-prep timeouts now **fail open to the real ACE/ACM treatment** instead of consuming the
  clinical click.
- The preflight state is retired before recursive/native treatment launch, so a callback that opens Narc Box, BVM,
  another modal UI, etc. cannot leave the old preflight behind.
- Provider stance ownership recognizes a treatment preflight only while its token and timestamp are genuinely live.
- Provider reconciliation now uses the real preflight start timestamp rather than waiting an additional four seconds
  after first noticing the stale Boolean.

B169's display-generation renderer ownership remains in place. B170 fixes the separate treatment-preflight lock exposed
by the RPT.
