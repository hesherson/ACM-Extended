## Manual plate-carrier replacement

An accepted manual Replace Plate Carrier action now calls the same provider-only Lower Head / supine reach-and-return controller used for laying the patient supine. It does not add another patient animation, progress timer, menu close or positioning log. Removal and automatic wake/transport restoration retain their existing separate paths.

The provider checks the initiating finite-pose epoch, locality epoch, native-treatment epoch, head-sequence generation and bounded presentation deadline before touching any animation. Late accepted equipment work can finish on the patient without forcing a stale reach onto a provider who has started another treatment. Existing movement/cancellation and bounded controller cleanup remain in use.

## Recovery cancellation on repositioning

An accepted front/back roll cancels the patient's pending or committed Recovery Position on the patient owner before the new animation is acquired. Cancellation retires the exact recovery token(s), clears recovery/head-tilt state as appropriate, and does not insert a supine animation over the roll. A rejected roll does not cancel recovery. Late begin/apply/commit callbacks cannot re-establish the retired position.

Native animation-state changes also detect front/back roll chains. The existing active-recovery watcher passively clears stale recovery after an external body-position change. Neither observer forces an animation. Chest preparation cancels recovery rather than keeping it for restoration on close. A side-lying recovery patient still requires a physical normalization roll even when the chest diagram classifies both positions as front.

The earlier B219 patient transition timing and slow blend are unchanged. Death-state preservation and the existing bounded recovery-death mitigation remain separate from living-patient roll cancellation.

## Validation scope

The focused B220 tests execute the production SQF through SQF-VM with explicit native-text-metric, animation, network, clock and control fixtures. They cover odd/even/empty catalogs, final-width fitting, multiple screen/UI metrics, accepted/rejected rolls, late callbacks, provider identity and exact reach/return cleanup. These tests are not live Arma rendering or multiplayer validation. The release verification extracts real PBOs, compares changed SQF with source and checks all component signatures/stamps.

The source bundle includes `tools/Deploy-ACME-B220.ps1` for a normal Windows HEMTT check/release and in-place deployment without removing source directories. Public version remains 1.2.4.1. No DEV changes are included.
