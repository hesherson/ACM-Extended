# ACM Extended 1.2.3 RC2: intervention priority and chest preparation

RC2 follows the first 1.2.3 CPR/BVM chest-access patch and addresses live animation ownership failures found during testing.

## Fixed ownership failures

### Direct Pressure versus CPR/BVM

Direct Pressure remains a persistent clinical state, but its decorative provider pose is lower priority than active intervention choreography.

- DP yields before chest-access preparation can animate.
- DP explicitly reads native `cprActive` and `bvmActive`; CPR does not depend on ACM's generic continuous-action flag.
- The short ACE/ACM launcher success event cannot resume DP while compressions or BVM remain active.
- Stale DP stop/reopen callbacks cannot hide CPR/BVM controls, force a crouch, or reopen the medical menu over a maneuver.
- New DP entry is rejected while the provider/patient is already in CPR/BVM.

### Semi-Fowler versus interventions

Semi-Fowler is now a posture preference beneath active clinical interventions.

- Temporary lowering requests use patient animation-lock priority 1.
- A higher-priority patient intervention lock prevents Semi-Fowler from taking collision/pin ownership.
- Resume waits until CPR, BVM and the patient-owner handoff deadline are all clear.
- Canceling chest preparation re-arms the normal deferred Semi-Fowler resume instead of leaving the patient flat.

## Plate-carrier custody across CPR/BVM

CPR and all BVM variants are one maneuver family for chest-access custody.

- One stable patient lease survives repeated CPR -> BVM -> CPR swaps.
- Provider-local handoff state and a patient-owner `serverTime` deadline both bridge the swap gap.
- Carrier restoration is blocked by active CPR, active BVM, a live lease, or the owner handoff window.
- The casualty owner no longer sends an asynchronous provider pose-stop after publishing chest readiness. The medic synchronously retires the exact preparation pose immediately before launching native treatment.
- A new intervention arriving during carrier return queues behind that short restore and retries automatically.

## One-click preparation UI

When physical chest preparation is required:

1. The first accepted click owns a unique preparation token.
2. Direct Pressure becomes animation-passive.
3. The medical menu closes immediately.
4. A noninteractive top-center **Preparing...** banner appears.
5. Additional clicks cannot enqueue another preparation.
6. Escape/F0 cancels only that token, removes its key handlers/banner, releases its exact lease and reopens the medical menu.
7. When preparation completes, the provider pose is retired synchronously and the requested native intervention starts.

## Animation timing

Carrier removal remains:

- lift: 0.70 s
- top hold: 0.04 s
- lower: 0.78 s
- total: 1.52 s

Carrier return is now faster:

- lift: 0.50 s
- top hold: 0.02 s
- lower: 0.55 s
- callback window: 1.07 s
- patient animation coefficient during this exact restore token: 1.60x

The coefficient is reset to 1.0 on normal completion and by an independent failsafe callback.
