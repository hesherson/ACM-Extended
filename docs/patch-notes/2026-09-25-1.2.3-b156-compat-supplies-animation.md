# ACM Extended 1.2.3 / B156

Based on published B155 (`0fc58f89b6db25df0a6586672e87c460c67a56a6`). B155's debug layout, original-color IV splay, texture containers, compatibility installation checks and dedicated-server chest-entry protections carry forward.

## ACE compatibility and coagulation

ACM intentionally overrides `getMedicationCount` with a scalar. Native ACM callers keep that contract. Extended effect readers accept both the scalar and current ACE's `[dose, effectiveness]` return; `getAllMedicationCount` now returns ACE-shaped summary rows over ACM medication records. A mission pain-function macro that expanded to a nonexistent symbol is corrected.

Coagulation rebuilds its calcium/hypothermia/acidosis base from input physiology, independently of the combined bleeding multiplier. Active patients retain the 0.2-second update. Treatment, ownership and fluid changes enroll immediately where hooks exist; registration and a 2-second discovery sweep catch other changes. Stable coagulation values have no periodic resend. Neutral recovery is broadcast exactly before retirement. Other physiology tick schedules are unchanged.

## Patient supplies and medication preparation

Treatment eligibility, tray counts and consumption use ACE's equipment-sharing order, including its medic-only mode. Patient and eligible vehicle item sources are supported where ACE's native inventory functions support them. This covers IV and airway tools, chest seals/decompression, thoracostomy, transfusion preparation, medication containers, warming/monitoring equipment and ventilator supplies. NRB oxygen follows the actual carried-cylinder owner; this does not add unsupported vehicle tank plumbing.

Reservations retain the original donor and vehicle. Rejection/cancellation refunds the same source once. Native IO collision refunds retain ACE's original item donor too. Reusable equipment keeps its existing custody rules; automatic ventilator recovery also handles a deleted AI donor. Medication vials retain the explicit Self/Patient/Vehicle selector and its lease. A locked draw cannot silently change source midway through preparation. Deferred source refunds wait for active inventory leases and apply their exact delta once on the source owner.

Repeated flush Save no longer debits twice, the last 0.01 mL respects the selected push duration, and native syringe preparation quantizes the dose before debiting its source. Changing syringe size updates the live plunger-to-volume conversion.

## Lifecycle and animation

Full-heal/reset invalidates delayed airway reflexes, collapse/blood workers, delayed hemolysis onset and mercy obtundation. Blast posture and temporary sprint falls release only their own episode, including reset and locality changes. Current episodes can resume after locality transfer, and an older worker cannot tear down its replacement. Death retains intervention evidence and equipment custody.

Moving preparation, carrier handling, patient rolling and exit animations use the shared 1.5x choreography rate. Clinical treatment and held-assessment durations remain real seconds. Frozen provider poses correct visible phase drift on observers and reject stale run/hold/exit packets. Patient motion speed belongs to the accepted animation lease.

The ordinary medical menu uses `ACM_GenericContinuous` where no other procedure owns the provider. Closing a menu releases its own generation; an older display's unload or exit callback cannot cancel a newer menu or treatment.

## Build and deployment

Build the complete fork with `hemtt release`; deploy all 14 PBOs with their matching signatures and server key. Keep the separate original ACM mod disabled. Restart clients and server and check **1.2.3 / B156** plus `[ACME COMPAT] OK` in a fresh RPT.

Source, executable SQF and Linux packaging validation are recorded in [B156 validation](../audits/2026-09-25-b156-validation.md). These do not replace an Arma dedicated-server test with two observing clients, a slow client, ownership changes and the actual modpack.
