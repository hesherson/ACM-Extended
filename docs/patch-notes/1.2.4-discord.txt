# ACM Extended v1.2.4 - Patch

:exclamation~1:Remember to repair your mod:exclamation~1:

## CPR / BVM
- CPR and BVM now properly share the same chest access sequence when a plate carrier needs to be removed
- Switching directly between CPR and BVM with middle mouse no longer puts the plate carrier back on the casualty between actions
- Plate carriers stay off throughout CPR -> BVM -> CPR transitions and are only restored once the intervention is actually finished
- CPR/BVM preparation now closes the medical menu and displays **Preparing...** while the animation sequence is running
- CPR and BVM can no longer be spam clicked during preparation and accidentally start overlapping animation sequences
- Canceling during preparation now returns you to the medical menu normally
- Plate carrier removal and replacement animations have been sped up
- Elevate Head/Semi-Fowler's now properly yields to CPR/BVM and only resumes after the intervention has completely ended

## DIRECT PRESSURE
- Direct Pressure now persists through CPR and BVM instead of being canceled
- Starting CPR or BVM temporarily yields Direct Pressure while the higher priority intervention is active
- Once CPR/BVM ends, the original Direct Pressure episode automatically resumes without needing to apply pressure again
- Direct Pressure stays yielded during CPR <-> BVM swaps so the pressure animation/effect no longer briefly reappears between actions

## CHEST SEAL / AUSCULTATION
- Chest Seal and Auscultation Flip now recognize the completed patient roll immediately instead of leaving the **Flipping...** button disabled longer than the animation
- Flip completion is retained even when the provider animation finishes first, preventing the minigame from immediately canceling when reopening or flipping again
- Chest interactions now keep the casualty supine when the procedure ends and no longer roll the patient back onto their stomach
- Chest Seal now transitions cleanly from the medic4 preparation animation into the hands-on-chest workspace while the minigame is open
- Exiting Chest Seal or Auscultation now returns the provider cleanly to a crouch
- Fixed a carrier removal/restoration retry path that could repeatedly reschedule itself and freeze the game during actions such as Check Breathing
- Delayed chest-access animations now recheck the patient state before continuing so dead, moved, or invalid patients cannot leave the procedure stuck

## SEMI-FOWLER'S / HEAD POSITIONING
- Semi-Fowler's patient and provider animations now start together instead of waiting on an extra provider-ready handshake
- Elevating or lowering the head no longer introduces a long dead-air delay between animation stages
- Lowering Semi-Fowler's no longer performs an unnecessary body roll after the casualty has already been set down
- The casualty remains supine after Lower Head, cancellation, CPR/BVM interruption, and other chest-access handoffs
- Repeated Elevate Head -> Lower Head cycles no longer leave stale patient animation locks behind
- Manual Semi-Fowler's support and Head Tilt-Chin Lift now release their hands-on action state cleanly when canceled or when the medical menu is reopened

## SHARED EQUIPMENT
- Medical treatments now correctly follow ACE Shared Equipment rules instead of only checking the provider's inventory
- Equipment can now be pulled from the patient or an eligible nearby vehicle when the configured ACE equipment-sharing mode allows it
- Shared equipment support now applies to IV/IO supplies, airway equipment, chest seals and decompression supplies, thoracostomy equipment, transfusion preparation, medication containers, warming/monitoring equipment, and ventilator supplies
- Canceled or rejected actions return reserved equipment to the same patient, medic, or vehicle inventory it originally came from
- Reusable equipment keeps its existing custody instead of being duplicated or refunded to the wrong inventory
- Medication vials retain the explicit Self / Patient / Vehicle source selector and can no longer silently change source during an active draw

## MEDICAL MENU / NARC BOX
- Fixed a treatment-preparation state that could leave the medical menu visible while most treatment buttons silently stopped responding
- Closing the Narc Box no longer leaves later treatment clicks blocked by a stale provider preflight
- Medical-menu treatment preparation is now presentation-only and can no longer consume a clinical action just because the weapon/crouch animation did not settle on the expected frame
- A newer treatment click safely replaces an older stale presentation sequence instead of being rejected
- Treatment preparation now clears its ownership before launching the real treatment, preventing Narc Box, CPR/BVM, Head Tilt-Chin Lift, and other modal actions from leaving the menu locked
- Direct Pressure or a tourniquet should no longer be required to make otherwise unresponsive medical-menu buttons start working again

## DEBUG / UI
- Version has been bumped to **v1.2.4**
- The debug overlay now reports **v1.2.4** and the current internal build
- Debug information scales consistently across resolutions and uses the full safe-area height
- Patient ownership, machine role, locality, and network information are grouped at the top of the debug display
- Debug text has been slightly reduced and the blue background made more transparent for better visibility during testing
