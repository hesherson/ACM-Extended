# B161 investigation and validation

## Incident evidence

Inspected the supplied `Arma3_x64_2026-09-25_13-02-39.rpt`, `.bidmp`, and both `FROZEN0`/`FROZEN11` minidumps. The RPT records watchdog failures at 13:11:21, 13:11:42 and 13:11:52, with no named SQF error immediately preceding them. The two Windows minidumps were captured ten seconds apart, each with 121 threads. Their retained execution addresses differ while the watchdog remains active. Raw address/stack-word inspection is not a symbolized stack trace and does not identify an exact SQF call site. The proprietary BIDMP content did not supply a readable script trace.

The incident loaded the development output at `F:\ACM-Extended\.hemttout\dev`. Deployment instructions therefore include `hemtt dev` as well as the in-place release deployment.

Reviewed the supplied ACM treatment and Check Breathing sources, ACE treatment/animation sources, and the Animate Rewrite core/ACE PBO scripts. No change to those external mods is required for these confirmed ACME defects. The dump alone does not establish that either external mod caused the recorded stall.

## Confirmed defects and changes

### Carrier retry can exhaust one frame

Both delayed carrier acquisition and restoration could receive an empty result from `patientAnimRequest` and queue a wait-until retry. Their readiness condition accepted an absent or expired competing lock. The retry called the same acquisition closure directly. A retired token is a permanent rejection even with no active lock, so the callback could append another ready callback repeatedly without letting game time or cancellation advance.

CBA's [waitUntilAndExecute](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3/blob/master/addons/common/fnc_waitUntilAndExecute.sqf) appends to a queue processed by the live `forEach` in [init_perFrameHandler](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3/blob/master/addons/common/init_perFrameHandler.sqf). The old behavior was reproduced with the actual B160 carrier closures and the actual retired-token arbiter branch. A test-only queue budget terminates the reproduction safely instead of hanging the test process.

Successful wait conditions now schedule the retry through a positive 0.05-second delay. This allows the frame to finish and invalidation/cleanup to run. Pending callbacks retain the existing patient locality and exact transaction checks. Delayed steps also re-evaluate patient eligibility; invalid animation cases use the existing nonanimated gear path. Check Breathing preparation explicitly aborts if its patient dies.

This is a demonstrated freeze-capable path, not conclusive attribution of the user's unsymbolized hang. RPT preparation/timer markers improve the next live trace if the problem persists.

### Flip loses the completion frame

The roll controller freezes at the authored 2.2-second sample and automatically exits after a short hold. Flip originally required that still-live stage-3 state. If the hold cleared before the flip handler's next observation, it waited until its 5.5-second deadline despite completed motion. A sparse frame could also skip the finite work state's last sample and leave stage 2 pending.

Roll completion now records its pose epoch. Both flip handlers accept only that exact completed epoch after the patient interval finishes. Sparse frames can complete an already-observed roll after its authored work time without seeking/replaying the ended animation. Existing patient dispatch gates and the bounded presentation fallback remain.
